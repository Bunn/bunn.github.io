Glassy Desk

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 7, 2026

The short version: Glassy Desk has no advertising trackers and does not require an account. Remote sessions connect directly to the computer you choose. Saved connections can sync through your private iCloud database, and RevenueCat processes anonymous purchase information so Pro access works.

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Glassy Desk for iPhone and iPad and Glassy Desk for Mac (“Glassy Desk,” the “App”) handle information. Fernando Bunn is the developer and data controller for the processing described here and can be reached at fernando@bunn.dev.

2. Information handled by the App

Saved machines and connection history

If you save a machine or start a connection, the App may store the machine name, hostname or IP address, port, username, MAC address, connection dates and duration, connection outcome, and your per-machine display and input preferences. This information supports saved connections, recent history, reconnection, and Wake-on-LAN.

Credentials

Passwords you choose to save are stored in the device Keychain and in an encrypted field in your private iCloud database so that saved connections can work across your devices. Passwords are used only to authenticate with the computer you select. They are not sent to the developer or RevenueCat.

Local network discovery

With your iOS or iPadOS Local Network permission, Glassy Desk uses Bonjour to find compatible Screen Sharing (VNC/RFB) and Glassy Desk services on your local network and resolve their names and network addresses. Discovery results stay on your device unless you choose to save a machine.

Fast Connection pairing and Mac permissions

Fast Connection uses the Glassy Desk Mac companion. Camera access on iPhone or iPad is optional and reads the pairing QR code locally; camera images are not uploaded. A code contains the Mac’s identity, network addresses, and temporary approval information.

The companion uses Screen Recording permission to capture the display and Accessibility permission to apply input you send. Clipboard text that you explicitly paste is sent to the Mac and placed on its system clipboard. Direct screen access checks discard their test frames.

A successful pairing creates a random, device-specific resume credential stored in the local Keychain. Fast Connection resume credentials do not sync between devices. The iPhone or iPad does not save the reusable Mac pairing password. The Mac stores a derived pairing-password credential and approved-device records locally. Remove a device in the Mac app’s Connections view to revoke its authorization.

Remote-session data

When you connect, Glassy Desk exchanges authentication data, screen updates, pointer events, keyboard input, and related session messages directly with the remote computer at the address you selected. The developer and RevenueCat do not receive or store your remote screen content or input. The operator of the remote computer and its network may process this data under their own policies.

Device logs

The App writes operational logs using Apple’s unified logging system for troubleshooting. Logs may include connection endpoints, status, selected settings, and error messages, but are not intended to contain passwords or remote screen content. These logs remain subject to Apple’s platform controls unless you choose to share diagnostics.

3. iCloud and on-device storage

Glassy Desk uses SwiftData and Apple CloudKit to store and sync saved machines, encrypted credentials, preferences, and connection history in your private iCloud database. Apple processes this data to provide iCloud. Availability and retention of synced data depend on your Apple Account, device settings, and Apple’s services. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Basic settings, such as whether onboarding has been completed, may also be stored locally on your device.

4. Purchases and RevenueCat

Glassy Desk uses RevenueCat to load purchase offerings, validate purchases, restore purchases, determine whether Glassy Desk Pro is active, provide subscription-management features, prevent purchase fraud, and understand purchase performance.

The App does not create a Glassy Desk account or give RevenueCat your name or email address. RevenueCat generates a random anonymous App User ID and may process purchase history, Apple receipt and transaction information, product and entitlement status, last-seen times, and limited technical information such as device type, operating system, locale, and currency. The developer can view anonymous customer and purchase information in RevenueCat’s dashboard.

Apple processes payments through the App Store. Glassy Desk and RevenueCat do not receive your full payment-card details. For more information, see RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. How information is used

The information described above is used only as needed to:

  • discover and connect to computers you choose;
  • save, sync, restore, and secure your connection settings;
  • show connection history and support reconnection;
  • provide, verify, and manage Glassy Desk Pro access;
  • understand optional usage analytics when you choose to share them;
  • diagnose failures, secure the App, and respond to support requests; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce the Terms of Use.

6. Sharing, analytics, and tracking

Glassy Desk does not sell personal information, show third-party ads, or track you across apps or websites for advertising. RevenueCat processes purchase information to provide and restore paid access.

Optional product analytics

On iPhone and iPad, Share Optional Analytics is off by default. When you enable it in onboarding or Settings, the App sends fixed events such as app opens, completed onboarding, connections, free-session usage, and purchase-flow outcomes to the developer’s analytics service hosted by Cloudflare. These events include app/build version, major OS version, a coarse iPhone/iPad device class, and limited outcome values. Event payloads do not contain persistent installation identifiers, hostnames, IP addresses, credentials, screen content, typed or pasted text, or precise session durations. As the network service provider, Cloudflare receives ordinary request metadata, including the source IP address, to deliver and secure requests.

With the same optional setting enabled, fixed usage milestones and a coarse free-session count band are attached to your existing anonymous RevenueCat customer profile to understand how usage relates to purchases. This profile has a persistent random identifier; its milestones are customer-linked, even though Glassy Desk does not provide your name or email. That identifier is not included in the Cloudflare event payload.

Turning the setting off stops new optional events, clears unsent local aggregate events and local milestone history, and requests removal of the optional RevenueCat attributes when the SDK next synchronizes. An upload already received by the server cannot be recalled. Existing aggregate events have no installation identifier with which to locate and delete one person’s events. Purchase verification, restore and transaction records continue independently of this setting.

Information is disclosed to the following recipients as needed:

  • Apple, for iCloud sync, purchases, platform services, and diagnostics you choose to share;
  • RevenueCat, for purchase/entitlement management and, when enabled, customer-linked usage milestones;
  • Cloudflare, for the optional aggregate analytics service and network delivery/security;
  • GitHub, when you download the Mac companion or its updates, with ordinary download-request metadata; and
  • the computers and networks you choose to access, which receive the data required for a remote session. A VPN such as Tailscale is a separate service governed by its provider’s policy.

Provider details: Cloudflare Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

7. Legal bases and international transfers

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing is based on performance of the agreement with you (providing the App and purchases), your choices and device permissions, legitimate interests in securing and improving the App, and compliance with legal obligations. Optional product analytics uses your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings.

Apple, RevenueCat, Cloudflare and GitHub may process information outside your country. RevenueCat states that its end-user information is stored in the United States and that it uses appropriate safeguards for international transfers. Their policies linked above provide current details.

8. Retention and your choices

Saved-machine information and history remain until you delete them in the App or they are removed through your iCloud controls. Because Keychain and iCloud data may survive removal of the App, delete saved machines inside Glassy Desk before uninstalling if you want their stored credentials removed. Apple may retain purchase records as required to operate the App Store.

RevenueCat retains purchase and anonymous customer information as needed to provide purchase history, restore access, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription or necessarily delete Apple or RevenueCat records.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. To make a request about Glassy Desk or RevenueCat data, email fernando@bunn.dev. Requests may require enough purchase information to locate an otherwise anonymous RevenueCat record.

9. Children

Glassy Desk is a general-purpose remote access utility and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
