FAQ

Do I need to install anything on my Mac?

For the best experience, install Glassy Desk for Mac. Standard VNC works without it.

Can I connect when I’m away from home?

Yes, with Tailscale. See the setup guide.

What’s included for free?

Timed sessions. Pro removes the limit, with monthly, yearly, and lifetime options.

Which devices can I use?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 26 or later. The Mac companion needs macOS 14 or later.
