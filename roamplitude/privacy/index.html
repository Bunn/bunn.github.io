THE SHORT VERSION

Your favorites stay with you.

Roamplitude has no account and no advertising or analytics SDK. Favorites and listening history are stored locally. Station searches and play-count requests go to Radio Browser, and audio streams connect directly to station providers. Those connections expose information such as your IP address to the receiving services.

1. Who we are

Roamplitude is developed and operated by Fernando Bunn. “Roamplitude,” “we,” and “us” in this policy refer to the app, this website, and its developer. This policy covers the information we handle when you use Roamplitude or contact us.

Contact us at fernando@bunn.dev with questions or privacy requests.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores your favorite stations and up to 50 recently played stations in local app storage. It also caches station-directory information, including station names, stream addresses, genres, countries, and published station coordinates, to make discovery more resilient when the directory is unavailable.

Roamplitude does not upload your favorites or local listening-history list to a developer-operated server. The app does not provide its own cloud sync. Local app data may be included in device backups managed by Apple, depending on your settings.

The globe uses station locations, not your device’s location. Roamplitude does not request access to your GPS location, microphone, contacts, or photo library.

3. Station discovery and Radio Browser

Roamplitude uses the community-maintained Radio Browser directory. The app contacts directory servers to load station listings and to search by station name, country, or genre. Search text and the filters you choose are included in these requests.

When you select a station to play, the app also sends its station identifier to Radio Browser’s play-count endpoint. This is separate from the listening history stored on your device.

Directory servers receive the request contents, your IP address, and an app user-agent identifying Roamplitude and its version. DNS providers handle the network lookups needed to find these servers. This information is needed to route and fulfill requests and may also be logged or used by the providers under their own policies.

Radio Browser is an independent service. Its operators control their logging, use, and retention of information. Avoid putting personal or sensitive information into station searches.

4. Live audio and station providers

Playing a station connects your device directly to the station’s stream provider and any delivery services it uses. These services receive your IP address and technical connection information, and can observe which stream you request and when. An IP address may reveal an approximate location.

Stations control their broadcasts, advertisements, stream metadata, and delivery infrastructure. They may use their own measurement or advertising systems. Their privacy practices are separate from Roamplitude’s; consult the relevant station or provider for details.

Some stations use unencrypted HTTP audio streams. Information sent over those connections may be visible to your network provider or others able to observe the connection. Roamplitude’s directory requests use HTTPS.

5. Apple services and diagnostics

Roamplitude uses Apple system features for playback, AirPlay, lock-screen controls, and audio routing. Apple also handles App Store distribution and any payment for downloading the app. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Apple may process App Store, device, backup, and diagnostic information under its own policies and your settings. If you allow Apple to share app diagnostics with developers, we may receive crash or performance reports to help fix problems. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Roamplitude does not include a third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK. It does not build an advertising profile, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

6. Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot issues, and maintain relevant support records. Our email provider processes the message as part of delivering and storing it. Please send only information needed for your request.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or browser storage to the Roamplitude pages, and the images and fonts are hosted with the site. GitHub and its delivery infrastructure may process IP addresses and request information to serve and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Following an external link connects you to a separate website governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Retention, deletion, and security

You can remove favorites in the app. Listening history keeps the 50 most recent stations, with older entries removed as new ones are added. Cached directory data is replaced when refreshed and may be removed by the operating system.

Deleting the app and its data removes its local favorites, history, and cache. Offloading the app can preserve its data. Backups may retain copies until you remove them through Apple’s backup settings. We cannot recover or remotely delete information held only on your device.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to address your request, keep necessary support records, or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion by email. Third-party providers determine their own retention periods; deleting Roamplitude does not erase their existing records.

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. No device, storage system, or internet connection can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and review what you include in support messages.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose which stations to play, remove favorites, stop playback, delete app data, and manage Apple backup and diagnostic-sharing settings. Live discovery and listening require connections to external services.

Where a legal basis is required, we process support information to respond to your request and for our legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the app, or to meet legal obligations. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to processing; and complain to a data-protection authority. Email fernando@bunn.dev to exercise rights relating to information we hold. We may need enough information to verify and locate your request, but do not ask for more than necessary.

Directory, streaming, hosting, email, and Apple services may process information in countries other than your own. Those services govern their own processing and any applicable transfer safeguards. Requests about their records should be directed to the relevant provider.

9. Children’s privacy

Roamplitude is a general-audience radio app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. Radio broadcasts may contain material unsuitable for children and are controlled by station operators.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app, its services, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated policy here with a revised date and provide additional notice or request consent where required.

For privacy questions, contact Fernando Bunn at fernando@bunn.dev.
