Privacy Policy

Your progress stays yours. Here is what Nova Swing uses to keep the game running.

Updated September 6, 2026

Who we are

Nova Swing is made by Fernando Bunn. This policy covers the app and these web pages. Questions or privacy requests: fernando@bunn.dev.

Your game saves

Progress, settings, credits, unlocks, and saved ghost runs are stored on your device. Supported progress also syncs through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. You do not need a separate Nova Swing account.

Gameplay analytics

The app sends gameplay events to our analytics service through Privatrace to improve balance and reliability. These include runs, scores, feature use, unlocks, and in-game economy events, with timestamps, a random session identifier, app version, device type, and operating system version.

These events do not include your name, email, advertising identifier, or precise location. The receiving server sees your IP address as part of the connection. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Apple and purchases

Apple handles iCloud and Game Center. When you sign in to Game Center, the app submits scores and achievements; your player name and scores may be visible according to your Game Center settings. Apple may share diagnostics with us if you allow it.

Apple processes optional purchases. RevenueCat helps validate transactions and deliver credits, using an app-specific identifier, purchase records, and device information. We do not receive payment-card details. Read Apple’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

This website and support

These pages add no analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare may process IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub’s policy and Cloudflare’s policy. If you email us, we use your address, message, and attachments to help with your request.

Your choices

Deleting the app and its data removes local saves. iCloud copies, backups, purchase records, and Game Center data are managed separately through Apple. Deleting the app does not erase records already held by service providers.

We keep information only as needed to provide and improve the game, resolve support requests, and meet legal obligations. Providers may process data outside your country and have their own retention rules. We use information to deliver requested services and maintain the game, with consent where required.

You may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, object to its use, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy authority. Email us for requests, including concerns about a child’s information. We can act on records we can identify and hold. Updates to this policy appear here with a new date.
