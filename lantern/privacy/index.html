LANTERN / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 1, 2026

The short version

LANtern looks at your local network and keeps what it finds on your device. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything about your network to us or anyone else. Apple handles the one-time purchase.

1. Who we are

LANtern is developed by Fernando Bunn. “LANtern,” “we,” and “us” refer to the app, this website, and its developer. Questions or requests: fernando@bunn.dev.

2. What LANtern does on your network

With your permission (iOS asks for Local Network access), LANtern finds devices on the network your iPhone or iPad is connected to. To do that it:

  • knocks on each address in your local network (ping and connection attempts on common ports),
  • listens for devices announcing themselves with Bonjour,
  • asks your router for the names it handed out, and
  • looks closer at devices that answered: which ports are open, the title of a device's web page, and the banner an SSH server shows.

These requests go only to devices on your local network. They identify themselves as LANtern. LANtern does not send what it learns to the internet.

3. What's stored on your device

LANtern remembers each network it has scanned and the devices it found there: names you give them, addresses, hostnames, hardware addresses when a device or router shares one, makers, models, the evidence behind each guess, and when each was first and last seen. It also keeps your preferences, such as the device view, accent and app icon.

This stays in the app's storage on your device. LANtern doesn't sync it to iCloud or any server. It may be included in device backups you make with Apple, depending on your settings.

4. The one-time purchase

Scanning is free. Unlocking every detail, theme and icon is a one-time in-app purchase made through Apple. Apple processes the payment and tells LANtern whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock on your devices and restore it later. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. What we don't collect

LANtern doesn't collect analytics, use advertising identifiers or tracking, ask for your location, contacts or photos, or require an account. We don't sell or share personal information.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us crash and performance reports. Those are handled under Apple's policies.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

You can forget a device in the app, and deleting LANtern removes everything it stored on your device. Because we don't hold any of it, there's nothing on our side to delete. Purchase records stay with Apple.

7. Email and this website

If you email us, we receive your address and message and use them only to reply. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which process connection information to serve it. These pages add no analytics or cookies.

8. Children

LANtern is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the update will appear here with a new date. Contact Fernando Bunn at fernando@bunn.dev. Purchases are covered by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement.
