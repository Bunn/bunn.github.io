GRIDSTORM / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version

Gridstorm doesn't collect any data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it doesn't connect to the internet. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

1. Who we are

Gridstorm is made by Fernando Bunn. Questions: fernando@bunn.dev.

2. What stays on your device

Gridstorm saves your campaign progress, unlocks, your ride, trail and color, your rider name for LAN games, and your settings. They stay in the app's storage on your device and aren't synced anywhere. They may be included in device backups you make with Apple.

3. Playing with nearby players

LAN games use Wi-Fi or Bluetooth to find and connect to nearby iPhones and iPads running Gridstorm, which is why iOS asks for Local Network and Bluetooth access. Players in the game see your rider name, ride, trail and color. Game moves go directly between the devices, never through a server or the internet.

4. Emailing us

The email option in Settings opens a message with the app version, iOS version, device model, language and region, and control setting, so you can see what's included before sending. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.

5. Apple

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us crash and performance reports. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which process connection information to serve it. These pages add no analytics or cookies.

7. Changes

Gridstorm doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included. If this policy changes, the update will appear here with a new date.
