Features

01

Keep the original

The real pass, not a screenshot.

Keeps the signed .pkpass with its design, fields, and barcode. Restore it to Apple Wallet any time.

  • Import from Wallet, Files, or Mail
  • Add notes without touching the original
02

Create collections

Group trips and events.

Build collections yourself or use on-device suggestions.

  • Search by place, issuer, note, or tag
  • Stack, carousel, grid, or list layouts
03

Memory map

See where your passes took you.

Routes come from pass details, not location tracking.

04

Photo memories

Attach photos to passes.

Add photos to passes and collections, and get yearly recaps and anniversaries.

  • Only the photos you pick are shared with the app
05

Share safely

Share the memory, not the barcode.

Share a card with the highlights. Sensitive details stay out.

  • No barcode or serial number
  • No notes, seat, balance, or raw pass fields
  • No original .pkpass data

Private by design

No account. No server.

Your passes stay on your devices and in your private iCloud.

iCloud

Private sync

Everything syncs across iPhone and iPad.

Lock

Device protection

Lock with Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode.

Backup

Encrypted export

Portable backups protected by a recovery phrase.
