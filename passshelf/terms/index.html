PassShelf organizes and archives eligible signed Apple Wallet .pkpass files. 4. Eligible passes and issuer limits

If Wallet sharing is unavailable, a compatible pass received in Files, Mail, or another app may still be importable.

Payment cards and secure-element credentials are not .pkpass files and cannot be archived by PassShelf. Some issuers may restrict sharing, restoration, transfer, display, or use. Archiving a pass does not extend its validity, preserve a seat or balance, guarantee entry, or override issuer rules.
