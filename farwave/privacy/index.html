THE SHORT VERSION

Your listening, explained.

Podcast libraries and imported music stay on your device. Pro radio favorites can sync with iCloud, and optional listener orbits use CloudKit presence. Discovery and playback connect to Apple, Radio Browser, and content providers. Apple and RevenueCat manage Pro purchases. Farwave does not require a separate account.

1. Who we are

Farwave is developed and operated by Fernando Bunn. “Farwave,” “we,” and “us” in this policy refer to the app, this website, and its developer. This policy covers live radio, podcasts, imported music, Farwave Pro, and support.

Contact fernando@bunn.dev with questions or privacy requests.

2. Information stored on your device

Farwave stores radio favorites, up to 50 recently played stations, station-directory caches, and app preferences. Podcast data includes followed shows, feed addresses, episode details, saved episodes, queue order, played status, listening progress, and audio you choose to download. Imported music, its metadata and artwork, and your private planet’s settings are also stored locally.

Podcasts and imported music do not sync through Farwave’s iCloud features. Local app data may be included in Apple-managed device backups, depending on your settings. Radio favorites have a separate iCloud sync feature described below.

The globe uses station locations, not your device’s location. Farwave does not request GPS, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access. Importing music gives the app access to the files you select; Wi-Fi transfer uses Local Network permission.

3. iCloud favorites and listener orbits

With Farwave Pro and an available iCloud account, radio favorites sync through a private CloudKit database associated with your Apple account. The app caches favorites on the device and syncs changes while active. Removing a favorite also syncs its removal. This feature does not upload your podcast library, imported music, or radio listening-history list.

When Listener orbits is enabled and iCloud is available, the app writes a reusable presence record to its public CloudKit database while active. It contains a heartbeat timestamp and technical record identifiers associated with the installation and an app-scoped iCloud user identifier. Other app clients read recent records to count active listeners and display orbits. The record contains no name, email address, station selection, or device location.

You can turn off Listener orbits in Settings to hide the orbits and stop your presence updates. An inactive presence stops contributing to the count after its lease expires; expiration of the count does not itself delete the stored CloudKit record. Apple processes iCloud information under its policies and your account settings.

4. Station discovery and Radio Browser

Farwave uses the community-maintained Radio Browser directory. The app contacts directory servers to load station listings and search by station name, country, or genre. Search text and selected filters are included in those requests. Selecting a station also sends its identifier to Radio Browser’s play-count endpoint, separately from the history stored on your device.

Directory servers receive request contents, your IP address, and an app user-agent. DNS providers handle network lookups. These services may log technical information under their own policies; their operators control its use and retention. Farwave’s directory requests use HTTPS.

5. Live audio and station providers

Playing radio connects your device directly to the station’s stream provider and its delivery services. They receive your IP address and technical connection information and can observe the stream requested and when. An IP address may reveal an approximate location.

Stations control their broadcasts, metadata, advertisements, and delivery infrastructure. They may use their own measurement or advertising systems. Some streams use unencrypted HTTP; information on those connections may be visible to network operators or others able to observe them. Consult the station or provider for its privacy practices.

6. Podcast search, feeds, artwork, and audio

Podcast search uses Apple’s public iTunes Search API. Your search text is sent to Apple. Opening or following a show, adding an RSS feed URL, and refreshing a library contact the publisher’s feed servers. Farwave also contacts artwork and audio hosts when loading images, streaming episodes, or downloading audio.

These providers receive your IP address, request details, and technical connection information. Publishers may use their own audience measurement or advertising systems, including in downloaded audio. A feed address you enter is stored in your local podcast library; avoid entering private credentials or sensitive information into searches or feed URLs.

Following a show does not automatically download its audio. You choose individual downloads, which are stored on your device. Unfollowing a show can retain saved, downloaded, queued, or in-progress episodes; remove downloads separately when you no longer want them.

7. Imported music and local transfers

Music imported from Files is copied into Farwave’s local library. Files may come from a device or a provider you select in Apple’s file picker. Farwave reads audio metadata and embedded artwork to organize the library; it does not upload your music to a developer-operated server.

When you start Wi-Fi transfer, Farwave opens a temporary HTTP server on your device for a computer on the same Wi-Fi network. The transfer page uses a session token to authorize uploads. Audio travels over your local network, not a Farwave cloud storage service. Local HTTP transfers are not encrypted; use a trusted network and share the transfer address only with people you intend to allow access. The server stops when the transfer session ends.

8. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple handles in-app payments. Farwave uses RevenueCat to load plans, validate purchases, determine Pro access, restore purchases, and provide subscription support. RevenueCat processes purchase history and an automatically generated app user identifier, together with technical request information needed to provide these services. Its dashboard also provides purchase and subscription reporting.

We do not receive your payment-card details. Farwave does not ask you to create a separate account or provide an email address for purchase identification, and it does not configure advertising identifiers or an advertising SDK.

See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and its Apple App Privacy disclosures. Purchase records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat after you delete the app.

9. Apple services and diagnostics

Farwave uses Apple features for background playback, AirPlay, lock-screen controls, CarPlay, widgets, file access, and iCloud. Widgets use shared app storage to display relevant radio information. Apple also handles App Store distribution and purchases.

Apple may process account, device, backup, and diagnostic information under its policies and your settings. If you permit Apple to share diagnostics with developers, we may receive crash or performance reports. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

10. Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments. We use them to respond, troubleshoot issues, and keep relevant support records. Our email provider processes messages for delivery and storage. Please send only information needed for your request.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. The Farwave pages do not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or browser storage. Images and fonts are hosted with the site. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and request information to serve and secure it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

External links take you to services with their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Retention, deletion, and security

You can remove favorites, unfollow shows, remove saved episodes and downloads, and delete imported music in the app. Radio history keeps the most recent 50 stations. Directory caches are replaced as refreshed. Unfollowing a podcast does not necessarily delete retained episodes or their downloads.

Deleting the app and its data removes local libraries and downloads; offloading can preserve them. Deleting the app does not itself erase synced iCloud favorites, presence records, backups, purchase records, or information held by external providers. To remove synced favorites, remove them in the app while sync is available. Manage Apple backups and iCloud storage through Apple’s settings, and contact us about information within our control.

We retain support correspondence as needed to address requests, maintain necessary records, or meet legal obligations. Providers determine retention of their own records. We cannot recover or remotely erase information held only on your device. No storage system or internet connection is guaranteed completely secure.

12. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose what to play or download, remove library items, turn off Listener orbits, end Wi-Fi transfer, and manage Apple account, backup, and diagnostic settings.

Where a legal basis is required, we process information to provide requested features and support, for legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the service, or to meet legal obligations. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to processing, and complain to a data-protection authority. Email fernando@bunn.dev about information we hold. We may need enough information to verify and locate your request.

Apple, RevenueCat, directory, podcast, streaming, hosting, and email providers may process information outside your country. Their policies govern their processing and applicable transfer safeguards. Contact the relevant provider about records it controls.

13. Children’s privacy

Farwave is a general-audience audio app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact fernando@bunn.dev so we can address it. Third-party radio and podcasts may contain material unsuitable for children.

14. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app, its services, or legal requirements change. Updates will appear here with a revised date, with additional notice or consent where required.

Contact Fernando Bunn at fernando@bunn.dev. Read the Terms & Conditions or return to Farwave.
