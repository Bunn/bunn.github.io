FAQ

What can I listen to offline?

With Pro, downloaded podcast episodes and imported music. Live radio and streaming need a connection.

Does my library sync between devices?

Radio favorites sync through iCloud with Pro. Podcasts, downloads, and imported music stay on each device. No account needed.

Does Farwave remove ads from radio or podcasts?

No. Stations and publishers control their own audio, including ads.

Which devices does it support?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 18 or later. Pro adds CarPlay and widgets.
